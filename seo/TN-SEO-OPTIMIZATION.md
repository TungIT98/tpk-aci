## TikTok SEO Fundamentals Applied

TikTok indexes: captions, hashtags, usernames, sounds, and engagement signals.
**Discovery pathway:** Hashtags (30%) + For You page algorithmic signals (70%)

Key principles:
1. First line of description is visible without "more" — make it count
2. Front-load primary keyword + hook in title and opening line
3. 3-5 niche hashtags + 2-3 broad discovery hashtags + 1 trending sound
4. Hashtags must match what users actively search, not just generic genre names
5. Hook must stop the scroll in first 1-2 seconds visually AND in the title
