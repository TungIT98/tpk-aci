# Audience Personas — TKP Content Agency

> Research output for TKP-12. Defines target audiences for both channels.

---

## Channel 1: Productivity Worker

### Primary Persona: "The Ambitious Overachiever"

**Demographics**
- Age: 28–40
- Education: Bachelor's–Master's degree
- Income: $55K–$120K annually
- Location: Urban/suburban USA, UK, Canada, Australia
- Work: Office workers, remote/hybrid knowledge workers, mid-level professionals
- Industries: Finance, tech, consulting, marketing, operations

**Psychographics**
- Feels chronically time-poor despite working long hours
- Consumes content during commute, lunch breaks, or "dead time"
- Seeking systems, frameworks, and shortcuts to reclaim 1–2 hours/day
-Aspires to be the "organized one" on their team
-Values credibility over hype — respects data and expert authority

**Content Preferences**
- "Systems that actually work" not motivational fluff
- Short-form (60–180s) preferred for mobile consumption
- 3–5 actionable steps, not abstract theory
- Clean, professional aesthetic (not gamer-style)
- Appreciates tools/tech recommendations (Notion, Obsidian, Raycast, etc.)

**Pain Points**
- Context switching between meetings destroys deep work
- Inbox zero feels impossible
- Always "on" but rarely productive
- Morning routine advice doesn't account for chaotic schedules
- Procrastination rooted in perfectionism, not laziness

**Motivation for Following**
- Save 30+ min/day → more time for family, side projects, rest
- Get promoted / earn more / stand out at work
- Stop feeling overwhelmed and reactive

**Avoid**
- Millennial hustle culture / "5am club" extremes
- Advice that requires 3 hours of setup
- Generic productivity content they've seen on every podcast

---

### Secondary Persona: "The Returning Professional"

**Demographics**
- Age: 35–50
- Re-entering workforce after parental leave, illness, or career change
- Needs to rebuild confidence and current skills

**Psychographics**
- Overwhelmed by how much has changed
- Wants productivity to feel manageable, not like another job
- Values safety and simplicity in recommendations

**Content Preferences**
- Slower-paced explanations
- Community/reassurance alongside tips
- Checklists over theory

## Channel 2: Gen Z Success

### Primary Persona: "The Ambitious Optimist"

**Demographics**
- Age: 18–28
- Education: High school → early career or university student
- Income: $0–$55K (students, early-career, gig workers)
- Location: Global (heavy in USA, UK, India, Philippines, Nigeria, South Africa)
- Social-first: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts native

**Psychographics**
- Deeply aspirational but skeptical of "hustle culture 1.0"
- Wants financial independence (not necessarily corporate success)
- Curious about side hustles, investing, personal finance, and skills
- Consumes content in rapid bursts; attention span is trained to scroll
- Identifies with "underdog" narratives and authenticity
- Reacts strongly to relatability and humor alongside value

**Content Preferences**
- Fast-paced editing with punchy text overlays
- Hook in first 2 seconds (question, bold claim, or shock stat)
- Vertical 9:16 format preferred
- Trend-audio-aware (uses trending sounds as vehicles)
- Quick tips, myth-busting, "things I wish I knew earlier"

**Pain Points**
- Doesn't know what career path to choose
- Sees peers succeeding on social media; feels left behind
- Financial anxiety: student debt, housing costs, unstable income
- "Should I go to university or learn to code?" paralysis
- Information overload — who's actually credible?

**Motivation for Following**
- Discover career paths and income ideas they've never considered
- Feel less alone in uncertainty
- Shortcuts and hacks that actually work in the real world

**Avoid**
- Preachy "when I was your age" tone
- Overly corporate or "enterprise" language
- Sounding like a LinkedIn influencer
- Content that requires long-form commitment

---

### Secondary Persona: "The Side Hustler"

**Demographics**
- Age: 22–32
- Has a full-time job but building something on the side
- Interested in: content creation, freelancing, e-commerce, reselling, crypto
- Income goal: $500–$5K/month side income

**Psychographics**
- Time-constrained — needs high-leverage strategies
- Self-educated via YouTube/TikTok
- Values proof (screenshots, income reports) over credentials
- Follows multiple creators in the space for triangulation

**Content Preferences**
- Income reports, case studies, behind-the-scenes
- Platform-specific growth tips (how to go viral on TikTok, etc.)
- Tool reviews and workflow setups

## Cross-Channel Insights

| Dimension | Productivity Worker | Gen Z Success |
|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Avg video length | 90–180s | 30–90s |
| Primary platform | YouTube, LinkedIn | TikTok, Instagram |
| Content format | Educational deep-dive | Trend-aware snackable |
| CTA style | Subscribe + watch next | Follow for more, save this |
| Aesthetic | Clean, premium | Vibrant, fast-cut |
| Best posting time | 7–9am, 12–1pm, 6–8pm | 12–3pm, 7–10pm |
| Key emotion | Relief + confidence | Excitement + possibility |
