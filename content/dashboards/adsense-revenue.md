## Factors Affecting RPM

| Factor | Impact on RPM | TKP Channels | Notes |
|--------|--------------|-------------|-------|
| Low CTR | Reduces RPM | — | Target CTR >4% |
| Low retention | Reduces RPM | — | Target retention >50% |
| Short videos | Reduces RPM | — | Ad inventory tied to watch time |
| Low audience demographics | Reduces RPM | Gen Z = positive | 18–34 demographic commands premium |
| Seasonality | ±20–40% swings | — | Q4 (Nov–Dec) highest CPMs |
| Geo (US vs. international) | US = 2–3× CPM | Both | International views dilute RPM |
| Demonetized content | Eliminates revenue | — | Avoid controversial topics |

## Seasonal CPM Calendar

> CPM fluctuates significantly throughout the year. Plan content and revenue projections accordingly.
> Q4 is the highest-revenue period — queue your best-performing content for November–December.

| Month | CPM Multiplier | Productivity CPM (est.) | Gen Z Finance CPM (est.) | Key Driver |
|-------|---------------|----------------------|--------------------------|------------|
| January | 0.85× | $3–7 | $7–17 | Post-holiday lull; New Year resolutions spike productivity searches |
| February | 0.80× | $2–6 | $7–16 | Lowest CPM month; short month, Valentine's Day ad competition |
| March | 0.90× | $3–7 | $8–18 | Recovery begins; tax-related finance content spikes |
| April | 0.95× | $3–8 | $8–19 | Tax season peak; spring productivity searches |
| May | 1.00× | $3–8 | $8–19 | Baseline; end of fiscal year for businesses |
| June | 0.95× | $3–8 | $8–18 | Summer slowdown begins |
| July | 0.85× | $2–7 | $7–16 | Low CPM; summer vacation period |
| August | 0.85× | $2–7 | $7–16 | Continued low CPM |
| September | 1.00× | $3–8 | $8–19 | Back-to-school; business year restart |
| October | 1.15× | $4–9 | $9–22 | Q4 ramp begins; holiday planning ads |
| November | **1.40×** | **$5–12** | **$12–28** | Black Friday / Cyber Monday — highest CPM week of year |
| December | **1.30×** | **$4–11** | **$10–26** | Holiday spending; December 18–26 CPM dip (lowest within Q4) |

> **Revenue planning rule:** Q4 (Oct–Dec) typically delivers 30–50% of annual AdSense revenue. A video published in November at the same views as June can earn 1.5× the revenue. Plan your highest-effort content for Q4.

### Q4 Rush Checklist

- [ ] Publish or unlist low-performing videos before Q4 (demonetization risk)
- [ ] Queue top-performing content for October–December
- [ ] Verify AdSense account is linked before October (payment threshold)
- [ ] Increase upload cadence in September to build momentum for Q4
- [ ] World Cup channel launch: target by September to capture Q4 ad spend spike — World Cup energy carries into November/December for sports-adjacent content
