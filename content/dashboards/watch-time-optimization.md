## Retention Optimization Opportunities

| Drop-off Pattern | Likely Cause | Recommended Fix |
|-----------------|-------------|----------------|
| High drop at 0–15s | Weak hook | Rewrite first 15 seconds — lead with the payoff |
| Drop at 30s | Pacing issue | Cut slow sections; add pacing cuts |
| Drop at 2–3 min | Lost momentum | Use chapter markers; signal "the main point is coming" |
| Mid-video plateau | Content gap | Re-examine topic selection; tighten script |
| Late drop | Video outstays welcome | Hard cap editing; end 10–20 seconds early |
