## Community Tab Strategy

YouTube Community tab is underused by most channels — but drives significant subscriber retention and algorithm signaling. Use it like a social feed: varied content, engagement prompts, behind-the-scenes.

## Content Types

### Poll Posts
- Ask audience opinion on topic related to upcoming video
- 2–4 options only (more = lower completion rate)
- Run 24–48 hours for maximum response
- Pin best poll results as follow-up comment

**Example**: "What's your biggest time waster at work? [Meetings / Email / Social media / Procrastination]"

### Image Posts
- Share a screenshot, tool setup, result, or graphic
- Must be landscape 16:9 or square
- Caption with engagement question

**Example**: "Here's my exact Notion weekly review template. What would you add to it?"

### Text Updates
- Short paragraphs — behind-the-scenes, updates, announcements
- Always end with engagement question

**Example**: "Just finished filming a video on the 2-minute rule. I genuinely think this changed my relationship with small tasks. Would you try it?"

### Video Clips
- Short 30s clips from long-form videos
- Share to TikTok + YouTube Community simultaneously

## Community Tab Setup Steps (Operator)

Once YouTube channels are live:

1. **Enable Community tab**: YouTube Studio → Customization → Layout → make sure Community tab is visible
2. **Set up post hold** (optional for launch week): Settings → Community → enable moderation hold — prevents spam but delays posts; turn off after launch
3. **Pin first post**: Pin the launch announcement post so new visitors see it
4. **Schedule posts in advance**: Use YouTube Studio's scheduling to queue Week 1 posts
5. **Respond to all comments within 24 hours** on launch week — early engagement signals to YouTube algorithm
