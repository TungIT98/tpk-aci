# Engagement Response Templates — TKP Content Agency

> 30 response templates for common YouTube/TikTok comment types, with tone variations for two channels.
> Use the Productivity Worker voice for TKP Productivity. Use the Gen Z Success voice for TKP Growth.

---

## How to Use These Templates

- Fill `[ ]` placeholders before posting.
- Adjust personality markers (emojis, slang, formality) to match current video tone.
- Do not respond to every comment — prioritize comments with high engagement potential (100+ likes, questions, genuine debate).
- Escalate threats, harassment, and hate speech to community moderation — do not engage.

## CHANNEL 1: TKP PRODUCTIVITY (Professional · Warm · Trusted)

### Questions

**Q1 — Tool Recommendation Request**
> "Great question! I actually have a full video on `[tool name]` coming `[this week/next month]` — it covers everything from setup to the exact workflow I use. Drop your email in the community tab and I'll ping you when it's live."

**Q2 — "What's your morning routine?"**
> "Thanks for asking! Mine shifts seasonally, but the non-negotiable pieces are: no phone for the first 30 minutes, a written priority list before I open email, and `[1 specific habit]`. I'll do a full deep dive on this in an upcoming video."

**Q3 — Clarification on a Framework**
> "Solid catch — happy to clarify. The key distinction is: `[concept A]` applies when `[condition]`, while `[concept B]` is better when `[condition]`. I actually prefer `[X]` for most people starting out because `[reason]`. Hope that helps!"

**Q4 — "Does this work for [specific job/industry]?"**
> "Depends on your context. For `[industry/role]`, the core principle still holds, but you'd want to adapt it by `[specific adjustment]`. I'd also suggest looking into `[related resource]` — they cover this niche really well."

**Q5 — Follow-up Question on a Past Video**
> "That video was from `[timeframe]`, and honestly my thinking has evolved slightly — I'd now add `[new insight]`. But the core framework still works. Want me to do a 'what I changed' follow-up?"

**Q6 — "Can you do a video on [topic]?"**
> "This is on the list — genuinely one of the most-requested topics. It's in the queue for `[month]`. In the meantime, the closest thing I have is `[existing video]` which covers `[related concept]`."

**Q7 — Opposite-Perspective Question**
> "Love this counterpoint. My honest take: `[answer with nuance]]` — but for someone at `[different situation]]`, `[alternative view]]` makes total sense. No framework is universal. What's your experience been?"

**Q8 — Overly Vague Question**
> "Can you say more about what you're trying to solve? Are you dealing with `[specific scenario A]]` or `[scenario B]]`? Once I know the context I can point you to the right video."

### Praise

**P1 — "This changed my life / best video ever"**
> "This genuinely made my day. If it helped you, the biggest thing you can do is actually apply one thing from this video consistently for 30 days — that's where the real shift happens. Would love to hear what lands for you."

**P2 — "I've watched this 5+ times"**
> "Honestly speechless. The fact that you're coming back to it means something in there is actually working for you — that's the whole goal. Bookmark it and revisit it quarterly; you'll notice you get something new each time."

**P3 — "Finally someone said this / you get it"**
> "This is exactly why I made this. The mainstream advice around `[topic]` is so [misleading/popular/overcomplicated] and I kept seeing people struggle. Glad it landed for you."

**P4 — "Taking notes / saved this"**
> "Best signal you can give me — it means the content is actually useful. Share it with one person who needs it and you've already multiplied the impact."

---

### Criticism / Pushback

**C1 — "This is obvious / common sense"**
> "Fair take. For someone who's already doing this consistently, yeah — it probably is basic. But the gap I see most often isn't knowing this, it's executing it under real pressure. Curious if you've found a way to make it stick in `[specific context]`?"

**C2 — "You're wrong about [specific point]"**
> "I respect the directness. My reasoning was `[X]]` — happy to be wrong if you have a source that contradicts it. Can you share what changed your view?"

**C3 — "Too vague / no actual steps"**
> "Completely valid critique — you're right that I stayed at the conceptual level here. I have a follow-up video in edit right now that gets into the `[specific tool/step]]` level. Want me to ping you when it's up?"

**C4 — "This only works for [privileged situation]"**
> "This is a real limitation and I should have addressed it in the video. For people with `[specific constraint]`, the adaptation would be `[adjustment]`. Adding this to my notes for the written summary."
