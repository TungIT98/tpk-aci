# Channel Personas & Content Pillars — TKP Content Agency

> Research output for TKP-14. Defines personas for both channels.

---

## Channel 1: "Productivity Worker" Persona

**Channel Name**: TKP Productivity *(final name TBD — short, memorable)*

**Tagline Concept**: "Work less. Get more done."

**Target Persona**: The ambitious professional who wants to be organized but struggles with modern work chaos.

---

### Tone of Voice

| Dimension | Channel 1 Voice |
|-----------|----------------|
| Primary register | Professional but warm — a trusted colleague, not a coach |
| Sentence style | Short, declarative. Avoids jargon. |
| Emotional register | Calm confidence — "here's what works" not "you need to change" |
| Humor | Light self-deprecation; relatable frustration moments |
| Authority signals | Data-backed, cites studies occasionally; shows tool setups |
| Avoid | Preaching, hustle culture, condescension, corporate speak |

**Example Hooks**:
- "You're not bad at time management. Your meetings are."
- "The 2-hour rule that transformed my focus"
- "I stopped checking email in the morning — here's what happened"

---

### Visual Style

- **Color palette**: Deep navy (#1a2744), warm white (#f8f7f4), gold accent (#c9a84c)
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif headers (Inter or DM Sans), readable body
- **Thumbnail style**: Minimalist — single strong visual (clock, desk setup, laptop), one bold text phrase
- **Face presence**: Presenter on camera optional; if present — professional casual (no tie, no hoodie)
- **B-roll**: Clean desk setups, coffee, Notion/Raycast screens, calm office environments
- **Motion**: Slow, purposeful cuts. No rapid montage unless for a specific comedic beat.

---

### Recurring Segments

1. **"System Setup"** (weekly)
   - "How I set up [tool] from scratch"
   - Format: Screen record + voiceover, 5–8 min
   - Example: "How I built my weekly review system in Notion"

2. **"Hot Take"** (bi-weekly)
   - Contrarian productivity opinion
   - Format: Direct-to-camera, 60–90s
   - Example: "Stop using to-do lists. Here's what to use instead."

3. **"Real People"** (monthly)
   - Interview or case study of a productivity system that actually works
   - Format: 10–15 min documentary style

4. **"Deep Dive"** (monthly)
   - Long-form analysis of a productivity book or framework
   - Format: 15–20 min essay-style narration

---

### 5 Content Pillars

| # | Pillar | Description | Example Videos |
|---|--------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Time Systems** | How to structure the day, week, and month for maximum output | "The 90-minute focus system I use instead of Pomodoro" |
| 2 | **Tool Stack** | Best tools for async work, notes, scheduling, communication | "5 tools that cut my admin time by 3 hours/week" |
| 3 | **Meeting Culture** | How to run fewer, shorter, more effective meetings | "I eliminated 6 hours of meetings per week — here's how" |
| 4 | **Mental Models** | Decision frameworks, prioritization systems, stress reduction | "The Eisenhower Matrix is wrong — here's what to use instead" |
| 5 | **Work-Life Integration** | Realistic routines for busy professionals, not 5am-clubbers | "My realistic evening routine when I work until 6pm" |

## Channel 2: "Gen Z Success" Persona

**Channel Name**: TKP Growth *(final name TBD)*

**Tagline Concept**: "Your next level starts here."

---

### Tone of Voice

| Dimension | Channel 2 Voice |
|-----------|----------------|
| Primary register | Energetic, direct, honest — a slightly older friend who's been through it |
| Sentence style | Punchy. Short sentences. Staccato rhythm. |
| Emotional register | Exciting but grounded — "it's possible, and here's how" |
| Humor | Self-aware, ironic, Gen Z–native humor (not boomer-approved) |
| Authority signals | Real results, real stories, no fake guru energy |
| Avoid | LinkedIn-speak, gatekeeping, "when I was your age", overly corporate tone |

**Example Hooks**:
- "The career advice I got at 22 that I wish I ignored"
- "I made $3K in one month doing THIS — here's the system"
- "Why your同龄人 (peers) are getting ahead — it's not luck"

---

### Visual Style

- **Color palette**: Electric violet (#7c3aed), hot coral (#ff6b6b), near-black (#111827), white
- **Typography**: Bold, condensed display fonts for text overlays (Bebas Neue style); clean body
- **Thumbnail style**: High contrast; bold claim text; often face + text; reaction faces work
- **Face presence**: Always on camera (personality is the brand)
- **B-roll**: Fast cuts, phone screens, city/street footage, coffee shops, laptop setups
- **Motion**: Quick cuts, 1–3 second shots, zoom effects, transitions on beat

---

### Recurring Segments

1. **"$X in Y"** (weekly)
   - Income/side hustle case study
   - Format: Income screenshot + breakdown narration, 60–90s
   - Example: "$2,400 in one month reselling — the exact system"

2. **"Career Truths"** (bi-weekly)
   - Honest career advice that contradicts mainstream wisdom
   - Format: Direct-to-camera, energetic, 45–90s
   - Example: "Why I dropped out of the career I spent 4 years training for"

3. **"Tool Talk"** (bi-weekly)
   - Quick tool/skill that creates leverage
   - Format: Fast demo + text overlays, 30–60s
   - Example: "5 free AI tools that replaced my $500/mo assistant"

4. **"POV" Series** (weekly)
   - Day-in-the-life or situational comedy tied to Gen Z experience
   - Format: POV-style storytelling, 30–60s
   - Example: "POV: you're about to make your first $1K online"

---

### 5 Content Pillars

| # | Pillar | Description | Example Videos |
|---|--------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Income Hacks** | Side hustles, freelancing, unconventional money-making | "3 side hustles you can start this weekend with $0" |
| 2 | **Career Strategy** | Job market navigation, salary negotiation, networking | "I negotiated a $20K raise — here's exactly what I said" |
| 3 | **Skill Stacking** | High-leverage skills (AI tools, coding basics, design) | "The one skill that 10x'd my productivity — no course needed" |
| 4 | **Money Mindset** | Financial literacy, investing basics, debt mindset | "I paid off $18K student debt in 14 months — not by being frugal" |
| 5 | **Life Systems** | Productivity for real life (not corporate), routines, focus | "My morning routine as a night owl who has to be at work at 8am" |
