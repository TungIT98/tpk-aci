## Channel 1: TKP Productivity

### Profile Picture
- **Design**: "TKP" wordmark in deep navy (#1A2744) on warm off-white (#F8F7F4) background
- **Style**: Clean sans-serif, DM Sans Bold. Minimal, no gradients, no drop shadows
- **Look**: Professional journal / premium tool brand feel
- **Shape**: Rounded corners to match YouTube avatar circle
- **Background**: Solid off-white or very subtle paper texture

### Channel Banner
- **Dimensions**: 2560×1440 px (YouTube safe zone: center 1546×423 px — keep critical elements here)
- **Background**: Navy (#1A2744)
- **Left/center**: Large "TKP PRODUCTIVITY" wordmark in white, clean sans-serif
- **Accent**: Thin warm gold (#C9A84C) horizontal line or arrow motif (workflow theme)
- **Optional overlay**: "Work less. Get more done." tagline in gold, smaller
- **Right side**: Content pillar tags in gold text: "Time Systems | Tool Stack | Mental Models"
- **Font**: DM Sans Bold / Extra Bold
- **Style**: Minimalist, magazine-editorial feel — calm authority

### Watermark
- **Size**: 150×50 px transparent PNG
- **Design**: "TKP" text only, in navy (#1A2744), 80% opacity
- **Placement**: YouTube places automatically in bottom-right of all videos
- **Style**: Same wordmark as PFP — consistent across all touchpoints

## Channel 2: TKP Growth

### Profile Picture
- **Design**: "TKP" wordmark in pure white (#FFFFFF) on electric violet (#7C3AED) background
- **Style**: Bold condensed style (Bebas Neue or equivalent). High energy.
- **Look**: Urban, Gen Z, street culture meets career empowerment
- **Shape**: Rounded corners to match YouTube avatar circle
- **Background**: Solid electric violet or black/violet gradient (max 2 colors)

- **Dimensions**: 2560×1440 px (same safe zone rules)
- **Background**: Deep black (#111827)
- **Left/center**: "TKP GROWTH" in electric violet (#7C3AED) or pure white, bold condensed font
- **Accents**: Hot coral (#FF6B6B) and neon yellow (#FACC15) geometric shapes (triangles, lines, circles) — bold, asymmetric layout
- **Optional overlay**: "Your next level starts here." in neon yellow, smaller
- **Right side**: Content pillar tags: "Income Hacks | Career Strategy | Skill Stacking"
- **Font**: Bebas Neue for display, Inter for any supporting text
- **Style**: Bold, street-culture meets tech startup — energetic contrast

### Watermark
- **Size**: 150×50 px transparent PNG
- **Design**: "TKP" text only, in white (#FFFFFF), 85% opacity
- **Style**: Same wordmark as PFP — consistent across all touchpoints

## Approved Design Direction (Reference)

**Productivity**: Clean editorial. Think Bloomberg, Notion brand page, Calm app. Minimal elements, premium feel. No clutter.

**Growth**: Bold street. Think Supreme drops, Off-White, Gen Z tech brands (Arc browser, Linear). Bold shapes, high contrast. No pastels.

---

## What to Avoid

- Gradients on Productivity (flat color only)
- Muted/desaturated colors on Growth (vibrancy is the brand)
- Comic sans, Papyrus, or decorative display fonts
- Blurry imagery or low-res assets
- More than 3 colors per banner
- Unlicensed fonts
