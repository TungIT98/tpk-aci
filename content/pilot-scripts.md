## Channel A: Productivity Worker
**Audience:** Office workers 25-40, focused professionals, "work smarter not harder" mindset
**Tone:** Calm authority, evidence-based, relatable office moments
**Format:** 60–90 second videos, 16:9 and 9:16 variants

---

### PW-01: "The 2-Minute Rule That Eliminates Procrastination"
- **Hook:** "There's a 2-minute task sitting in your inbox right now that's costing you hours of anxiety."
- **Intro:** Most of us ignore small tasks until they become massive stressors. But what if you could stop that spiral in under 2 minutes?
- **Body:**
  1. **The rule:** If a task takes less than 2 minutes, do it immediately. Don't add it to your list.
  2. **The neuroscience:** Each unfinished task consumes "cognitive RAM." Finishing it frees your working memory.
  3. **Real examples:** Replying to a quick email, filing a receipt, adding a calendar invite, sending a five-word Slack reply.
- **Outro:** Next time you see a 2-minute task, ask: "Is this worth my anxiety for the next week?" Do it now. Subscribe for more productivity science.
- **Hailuo visual:** Clean desk, hand reaches in to quickly file a paper. Close-up of inbox, items rapidly checkmarked. Calm face, exhale. Clean desk, wide establishing shot.

---

### PW-02: "Why Your Morning Meeting is Destroying Your Afternoon Output"
- **Hook:** "Your 9am meeting isn't just stealing an hour — it's killing your entire afternoon's best thinking."
- **Intro:** Scientists call it "attention residue." Here's what it is and why back-to-back meetings are your biggest productivity killer.
- **Body:**
  1. **Attention residue:** When you switch tasks, part of your brain stays on the previous task — reducing quality on the new one by up to 40%.
  2. **Meeting stacking:** Three consecutive meetings = 2-3 hours of fragmented, low-quality work after.
  3. **The fix:** Block 90-minute "focus blocks" after meetings. Treat them like a standing calendar appointment.
- **Outro:** Your calendar is a productivity tool, not a to-do list. If a meeting doesn't have a clear output, decline it. More insights — subscribe.
- **Hailuo visual:** Calendar view with color-coded meeting blocks. Transition to: person at desk, looks tired, head in hands. Then: same person, focus headphones on, productive flow state. Clean cut to calm face.

### PW-03: "The Sunday Night Ritual That Makes Monday Mornings Effortless"
- **Hook:** "What separates people who dread Mondays from those who dominate them? It happens the night before."
- **Intro:** A 20-minute Sunday ritual can eliminate Monday morning decision fatigue and give you an unfair advantage before 9am.
- **Body:**
  1. **Brain dump (5 min):** Write every unfinished task, worry, and commitment onto paper. Get it out of your head.
  2. **Outfit + bag (5 min):** Lay out Monday's clothes. Pack your bag. Eliminate 10 decisions before they happen.
  3. **3 Priorities (10 min):** Identify your 3 most important outcomes for the week. Write them somewhere visible.
- **Outro:** 20 minutes Sunday saves 2 hours of Monday morning scrambling. Do the work the night before. Subscribe for weekly science-backed productivity tips.
- **Hailuo visual:** Timelapse of evening — desk cleared, clothes laid out, notepad with 3 priorities. Sunrise, coffee in hand, calm person walks out door confidently.

### PW-04: "Stop Doing This — The Productivity Habit That's Actually Making You Slower"
- **Hook:** "Checking your email first thing in the morning feels productive. It's actually making you reactive all day."
- **Intro:** Most workers start their day by handing control of their attention to other people's priorities. Here's the data on why that backfires.
- **Body:**
  1. **The reactive trap:** Email is designed to respond to others. Starting with it puts you in a reactive posture before you've defined your own goals.
  2. **The cortisol effect:** Stressful messages first thing spike cortisol, reducing creative thinking for hours.
  3. **The "First Hour" rule:** Use your first 60-90 minutes for deep work. Process email after your most important task is done.
- **Outro:** You wouldn't let a stranger rearrange your desk. Stop letting their emails rearrange your day. Subscribe for more anti-habits that actually work.
- **Hailuo visual:** Phone screen with red notification badges, hand picks it up. Transition: same person, laptop open to blank document, coffee, calm morning light. Phone face-down in background. Wide establishing shot of peaceful home office.

### PW-05: "The Evidence-Based 90-Minute Work Rhythm Top Performers Use"
- **Hook:** "Your brain isn't designed to work for 8 straight hours. Ultradian rhythms explain why you crash at 2pm."
- **Intro:** Elite performers don't work longer — they work in rhythms that match their biology. Here's the 90-minute rhythm that doubles output.
- **Body:**
  1. **Ultradian rhythms:** Your brain operates in 90-minute cycles of higher and lower alertness — like circadian rhythms but shorter.
  2. **Work in sprints:** 90 minutes of focused, distraction-free work → 20-minute break. Repeat. This is how athletes train — and how top performers work.
  3. **The break matters:** True breaks (no screens, no social media) allow your pre-frontal cortex to consolidate and restore. Walking, stretching, staring out a window.
- **Outro:** Stop forcing 8-hour marathons. Work in 90-minute sprints and take real breaks. Your brain will thank you. Subscribe for more performance science.
- **Hailuo visual:** Timer visual — 90-minute countdown in center. Person in flow state, typing rapidly. Clock ticking. 20-minute break: person walking outside, coffee, relaxed smile. Cycle repeats. Split screen showing productive vs. distracted comparison.

### GZ-04: "I Turned My Anxiety Into My Biggest Asset — Here's the Framework"
- **Hook:** "High-functioning anxiety people: your brain isn't broken. It's calibrated for a world your ancestors lived in."
- **Intro:** If you've been told to "just calm down" — this is for you. Your anxiety isn't a bug, it's a feature that's been misdirected. Here's how to redirect it.
- **Body:**
  1. **The neuroscience:** Anxiety = hyperactive prefrontal cortex + heightened threat detection. It's your brain trying to PROTECT you. The problem is modern stressors feel like existential threats.
  2. **Channel it into systems:** Anxious people are exceptional at contingency planning. Use that energy to build backup plans, not worry about them.
  3. **The 5-4-3-2-1 audit:** When anxiety spikes, name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you feel, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. Resets the nervous system in 60 seconds.
- **Outro:** Your anxiety is a superpower when you understand the operating system. Drop "SUPERPOWER" if you resonate. Following is the best investment you make this year.
- **Hailuo visual:** Person in room, sudden close-up of anxious expression, heartbeat visual. Transition: same person breathing slowly, sunset light through window. Then: organized desk, clear planner, calm focused eyes. Warm golden hour tone throughout.

### GZ-05: "The 'Rich Person' Money Habits That Take 5 Minutes a Day"
- **Hook:** "I spent $400/month on coffee and didn't realize I was poor until I did this 5-minute math."
- **Intro:** Wealth isn't about how much you earn. It's about three daily behaviors that compound over years. Here's the exact 5-minute daily money system.
- **Body:**
  1. **Track one number:** Not your full budget — just your "fun spending" number. What you spend on coffee, Ubers, random purchases. Awareness reduces it by 23% without restriction.
  2. **The 24-hour rule:** Before any non-essential purchase over $50, wait 24 hours. If you still want it after sleeping on it, reconsider. Most impulse buys disappear.
  3. **Automate savings last:** Save AFTER expenses, not before. When the savings account has a specific job (name it: "trip fund", "upgrade fund"), saving feels like progress, not sacrifice.
- **Outro:** 5 minutes a day on your finances = 5 hours a year of clarity. Your future self will thank you. Follow for weekly money breakdowns that actually apply to your life.
- **Hailuo visual:** Phone screen showing spending tracker app. Coffee cup and calculator. Transition: piggy bank, then investment app with growing graph. Person smiling at phone with coffee, confidence. Urban rooftop morning shot.

```js
const gen = new ScriptGenerator();

const topics = [
  'The 2-Minute Rule That Eliminates Procrastination',
  'Why Your Morning Meeting is Destroying Your Afternoon Output',
  // ...
];

for (const topic of topics) {
  const script = await gen.generateScript({ topic, channel: 'productivity_worker' });

}
```
