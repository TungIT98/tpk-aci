## Content Angle Recommendations

1. **Control release framework:** Specific steps to let go without losing quality
2. **Common delegation mistakes:** Dumping tasks without context, micromanaging, not following up
3. **Outcome vs process:** Focus on "what" not "how"
4. **Psychological angle:** Why it's hard to delegate (perfectionism, control, fear)
5. **ROI angle:** Time saved = more strategic work = promotion faster

## Content Director Notes (Script Direction)

- **Script format:** Hook (relatable manager pain) → 3 delegation rules → transformation outcome
- **Length:** 45–75 seconds
- **Tone:** Peer-to-peer, empathetic — "I used to do this too" over lecturing
- **Key differentiator:** Give actual tactical rules (e.g., "always explain WHY, not just WHAT")
- **Series opportunity:** Could extend to "feedback delegation," "decision delegation," etc.
