## Content Director Notes (Script Direction)

- **Script format:** Recognition → validation ("this is not your fault") → 3 concrete recovery steps → encouragement
- **Length:** 60–90 seconds
- **Tone:** Warm, empathetic, non-judgmental — must feel safe, not preachy
- **Key differentiator:** Be specific and actionable. Don't just say "take a break"
- **Sensitive note:** Avoid triggering language. Focus on empowerment and recovery
- **CTA:** "Tag someone who needs to hear this 💛" — drives sharing and comments
