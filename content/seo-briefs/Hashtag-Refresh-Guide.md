## Why Refresh Matters

Trending hashtags shift week-to-week, especially on TikTok and Instagram Reels. The hashtags in each SEO brief were accurate at time of writing (2026-03-25). A pre-upload refresh ensures the video goes live with the most current discovery terms — maximizing Day 1 reach.

**Frequency:** Refresh hashtags **every 7 days** for all videos scheduled within that window.

---

## How to Refresh — Step by Step

### Step 1: Check TikTok Search Autocomplete (1 min)

1. Open TikTok, go to Search
2. Type your primary keyword (e.g., "side hustle", "time blocking")
3. Note the autocomplete suggestions — these are live trend signals
4. Note any hashtags that appear in top results

### Step 2: Check YouTube Tags Suggestion (1 min)

1. Go to YouTube, start typing your video title
2. Look at YouTube's autocomplete — these reflect real search volume
3. Note any additional keyword patterns YouTube surfaces

### Step 3: Cross-Reference with Google Trends (2 min)

1. Go to Google Trends (trends.google.com)
2. Search your primary keyword
3. Check "Search topics" and "Related queries" — note rising terms
4. Rising queries = new hashtag opportunities

### Step 4: Apply the 3+2+1 Rule

For **each video upload**, apply:

| Type | Count | Source | Example |
|------|-------|--------|---------|
| Broad (high volume) | 3 | Google Trends + keyword research | #productivity, #careeradvice |
| Niche (specific) | 2 | TikTok autocomplete + brief | #timemanagement, #mondayroutine |
| Trending (timed) | 1 | TikTok Discover page this week | Current viral sound hashtag |

**Do not exceed 5 hashtags on TikTok** — more reduces reach, not increases it.

## Per-Platform Quick Reference

### TikTok
- 3–5 hashtags total
- Always include at least 1 trending (from Discover page)
- Order: niche first, broad last
- Check: are any of your hashtags on the FYP (For You Page) banned list?

### YouTube (Long-form)
- Tags field: 10–12 comma-separated keywords (not hashtags)
- Hashtags in description: auto-extracted, max 15
- Use `#` format only in the description, not in title

### Instagram Reels
- 10–20 hashtags (optimal range per Instagram algorithm)
- Mix of broad + niche + location (if relevant)
- Update with seasonal shifts (e.g., `#spring2026` in April)

### LinkedIn (repurposed videos)
- 3–5 hashtags, career/industry specific
- Examples: `#Leadership #CareerDevelopment #FutureOfWork`
- Refresh quarterly — LinkedIn trends are slower

## TikTok Trending Sound Strategy

Hashtags alone aren't enough — **sounds drive TikTok distribution more than hashtags**.

Before each upload:
1. Open TikTok and note the top 5 trending sounds in your niche
2. If a trending sound is relevant to your video topic, use it as the audio
3. Add the sound's associated hashtag alongside your keyword hashtags

**April 2026 Sound Themes to Watch:**
- Productivity: "get ready with me" edits, lo-fi beats, ambient focus music
- Gen Z: Reaction audio, "POV" format sounds, income-story narration tracks
