## Music Recommendations

### Productivity Trailer
- Mood: Calm, ambient, premium — not upbeat
- YouTube Audio Library suggestions: "Meditation" or "Focus Flow" tracks
- Avoid: Beat drops, high energy, Gen Z music
- Length needed: ~52 seconds

### Growth Trailer
- Mood: Energetic, beat drop at end, high energy throughout
- YouTube Audio Library suggestions: Upbeat lo-fi hip-hop, energetic electronic
- Must: Have a clear beat drop at 0:50–0:56
- Length needed: ~50 seconds
