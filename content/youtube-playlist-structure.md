## Why Playlists Matter for SEO

1. **Suggested video algorithm**: YouTube uses playlist context to determine which videos to suggest. A viewer who finishes "The 2-Minute Rule" will be prompted with the next video in the "Time Mastery Foundations" playlist.
2. **Watch time multiplier**: Playlist autoplay increases session duration — the #1 ranking factor for YouTube search.
3. **Search discoverability**: Playlist titles appear in YouTube search results, capturing keyword queries that video titles alone miss.
4. **Channel authority signals**: Organized playlists signal topical authority to YouTube's algorithm, improving overall channel ranking.

**Rule**: End every video with a playlist CTA — "If you found this useful, the full [Playlist Name] series is linked below."

## End-Screen and Cards Strategy

For each video, add:
1. **End screen**: Link to the NEXT video in the playlist (not just the channel page)
2. **Info card** (4:00–6:00 mark): Link to the playlist this video belongs to
3. **Playlist CTA**: "If you found this useful, watch the full [Playlist Name] series next"

**Playlist end-screen pairing**:
- Last video of each playlist → end screen links to first video of SAME playlist (loop)
- All other videos → end screen links to next video in playlist
