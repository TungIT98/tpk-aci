## Risk: YouTube Dependency

**Current:** 100% YouTube revenue (once YPP approved)

1. Algorithm changes reduce organic reach

3. Adpocalypse-style revenue drops
4. Channel suspension or strike

**Mitigation:** Target — no single platform >60% of total revenue by month 18.
