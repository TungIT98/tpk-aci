# Brand Identity Guidelines — TKP Content Agency

> Design output for TKP-21. Brand identity for both channels.

---

## Brand Architecture

**Parent Brand**: TKP (The Knowledge Project — abbreviated)

Both channels exist under the TKP umbrella:
- **TKP Productivity** — The professional, systems-focused channel
- **TKP Growth** — The aspirational, Gen Z-focused channel

**Relationship**: Separate identities that can cross-promote. A viewer of one may discover the other. Both benefit from the parent TKP brand credibility.

---

## Channel 1: TKP Productivity

### Brand Essence
**Who it is**: The organized, calm, data-informed guide for knowledge workers who want systems that actually work in the real world.

**What it stands for**: Evidence-based productivity. Systems over willpower. Realistic routines. Smart tools.

**What it's against**: Hustle culture, toxic productivity, advice that only works for 22-year-old students, performative organization.

### Visual Identity

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Logo | "TKP" wordmark — clean sans-serif, navy (#1A2744) |
| Channel colors | Navy, warm gold, off-white |
| Typography | DM Sans (primary), Inter (body) |
| Icon | Minimalist clock or workflow arrow — single-color, no gradients |
| Photography style | Real office environments, not staged; natural lighting preferred |
| Illustration style | Flat, minimal line illustrations; no 3D |

### Tone of Voice
- Calm authority — "here's what the research shows"
- Warm but direct — no fluff, no corporate speak
- Relatable frustration — acknowledges the chaos, then solves it
- Credible — cites sources occasionally, shows the tools working

**Sample copy:**
- "I stopped using to-do lists and my stress dropped overnight. Here's why."
- "The 90-minute focus system that replaced my 4-hour work blocks."

### Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Navy | `#1A2744` | Primary text, logo, headers |
| Off-white | `#F8F7F4` | Backgrounds, thumbnails |
| Warm gold | `#C9A84C` | Accents, CTAs, highlights |
| Slate gray | `#64748B` | Secondary text, metadata |

### Logo Usage Rules
- **Minimum size**: 24px height
- **Clear space**: 1× the logo height on all sides
- **On dark backgrounds**: Use white/light variant
- **Never**: Rotate, stretch, recolor, or add effects to the logo

### On-Brand vs. Off-Brand Examples

| On-Brand | Off-Brand |
|----------|-----------|
| Clean desk, coffee, notebook | Chaotic office with papers everywhere |
| Screenshot of a working Notion setup | Generic stock photo of person at laptop |
| Direct eye contact, calm smile | Stressed expression, furrowed brow |
| Gold accent text on navy | Rainbow or neon gradient |

## Channel 2: TKP Growth

### Brand Essence
**Who it is**: The energetic, honest, no-BS guide for young people building their first real career or income stream.

**What it stands for**: Real results. Honest advice. Side hustles that actually work. Financial empowerment. Breaking rules that don't serve you.

**What it's against**: LinkedIn-speak, gatekeeping, "pay your dues" mentality, predatory guru culture, hustle-as-identity.

### Visual Identity

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Logo | "TKP" wordmark — bold condensed style, white on black |
| Channel colors | Electric violet, hot coral, neon yellow, deep black |
| Typography | Bebas Neue (display), Inter (body) |
| Icon | Lightning bolt or upward arrow — dynamic, single-color |
| Photography style | Street photography feel, urban environments, authentic (not curated) |
| Illustration style | Bold shapes, high contrast, minimal detail |

### Tone of Voice
- Confident but honest — "here's what I actually did"
- Energetic delivery — high-paced, Gen Z-native
- Rule-questioning — "actually, you don't need to do it that way"
- Proof-driven — shows numbers, screenshots, results, not just opinions

**Sample copy:**
- "The career advice I got at 22 that I wish I had ignored instead."
- "$3,200 in one month. Here's exactly how."

### Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Deep black | `#111827` | Backgrounds, thumbnails |
| Electric violet | `#7C3AED` | Primary accent, logo background |
| Hot coral | `#FF6B6B` | Secondary accent, alerts |
| Neon yellow | `#FACC15` | Highlights, CTAs, energy accents |
| Pure white | `#FFFFFF` | Primary text on dark |

### Logo Usage Rules
- **Minimum size**: 20px height
- **Clear space**: 0.5× the logo height on all sides
- **On dark backgrounds**: Default (white logo on dark is brand)
- **Never**: Pastel or muted variants; the energy is in the vibrancy

### On-Brand vs. Off-Brand Examples

| On-Brand | Off-Brand |
|----------|-----------|
| Excited expression, big energy | Neutral, bored, or overly serious |
| Dark background, neon text | Light pastel backgrounds |
| ALL CAPS bold claim | Small, quiet text |
| Real payment screenshot | Generic stock money photo |
| Phone held up showing result | Corporate headshot |

## Cross-Channel Elements

### TKP Parent Brand Usage

When displaying the parent TKP brand (e.g., in cross-promotion):
- Use the **TKP Productivity** color palette (navy/gold) as the "neutral" version
- Both channel logos include "TKP" prefix — consistent wordmark across both
- Cross-promotion graphic: Dark (Gen Z) + Light (Productivity) side by side = "Two channels, one mission"

### Shared Brand Values (Both Channels)

1. **Honesty over hype** — We don't promise miracles. We show real work.
2. **Evidence over opinions** — Real data, real case studies, real results.
3. **Systems over willpower** — Sustainable frameworks, not unsustainable hero efforts.
4. **Respect for the audience** — Smart viewers who can handle nuance and complexity.

### Social Media Profile Guidelines

| Platform | TKP Productivity Handle | TKP Growth Handle |
|----------|----------------------|--------------------|
| TikTok | @tkpproductivity | @tkpgrowth |
| YouTube | TKP Productivity | TKP Growth |
| Instagram | @tkpproductivity | @tkpgrowth |
| LinkedIn | TKP Productivity | TKP Growth |
| Twitter/X | @tkpproductivity | @tkpgrowth |

### Content Disclaimer (Both Channels)

When discussing financial results or income:
- State clearly: "Results shown are not typical. Your results will vary."
- Comply with platform-specific disclosure rules (TikTok #ad, YouTube sponsor tags, etc.)
- Never imply guaranteed outcomes
