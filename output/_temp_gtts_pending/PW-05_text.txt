Your brain isn't designed to work for 8 straight hours. You're not a machine. And that 2pm crash? It's not about lunch. It's biology. Your brain operates in 90-minute cycles called ultradian rhythms. Think of them like shorter circadian rhythms. Every 90 minutes, your alertness naturally dips — your brain needs a reset. Athletes know this. They train in sprints, then rest.
