Your 9am meeting isn't just stealing an hour — it's killing your entire afternoon's best thinking. Scientists call it 'attention residue.' When you switch from a meeting back to your work, part of your brain is still back in that meeting room. Studies show this reduces your cognitive performance on the new task by up to 40 percent.
