# TKP ACI

## Company Overview

TKP ACI is a video production automation company leveraging Hailuo AI for video generation and browser-based publishing to YouTube and TikTok.

## Mission

Build a "zero-human" video production pipeline where AI agents handle:
1. Script writing
2. Video generation (Hailuo AI)
3. Platform publishing (YouTube, TikTok)

## Goals

1. Generate 1000 videos in 30 days
2. Launch 2 YouTube channels
3. Achieve YouTube Partner Program eligibility
4. Build engaged audience on TikTok
