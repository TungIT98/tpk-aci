## Visual Language

### Costume Standards
- **Heroine:** Flowing Hanfu (red/gold/white), elaborate hairpins, sword
- **Hero:** Dark robes, jade ornaments, sword or staff
- **Villain:** Black/dark red armor, menacing aura, grotesque features
- **Master:** Simple white/grey robes, wise appearance, subtle power

### Setting Standards
- **Temples:** Misty mountains, tiled roofs, cherry blossoms, stone pillars
- **Battlefields:** Desolate plains, broken swords, blood-red sky
- **Palaces:** Golden pillars, red walls, silk curtains, lanterns
- **Forests:** Ancient trees, bamboo groves, mystical fog, glowing spirits

### Camera Movements for Xianxia
- [Push in] — For emotional moments, character revelations
- [Pan right/left] — Battle choreography, armies clashing
- [Tracking shot] — Following a character through a temple
- [Pull out] — Revealing vast scale (armies, landscapes)
- [Shake] — For impact moments, explosions, sword clashes
