# Content Engagement Skill

## Viral Content Architecture

### The 3-Second Hook

On TikTok/YouTube Shorts, you have **3 seconds** to stop the scroll. If you don't capture attention immediately, viewers scroll past.

**High-Performing Hook Patterns:**

1. **Mystery/Hook**
   - "She found the sword and couldn't believe what happened next..."
   - "The secret they never wanted you to see..."
   - "Part 1 of the Trieu Tien saga..."

2. **POV Hook**
   - "POV: You're the sword saint's daughter"
   - "POV: You just joined the Nine Deaths Sect"
   - "POV: You have cultivation talent but no one believes"

3. **Emotional Hook**
   - "This scene made 1M people cry"
   - "The moment that changed everything for her"
   - "She trained for 10 years for this..."

4. **Action Hook**
   - "Watch the sword technique that broke the internet"
   - "The fight scene that took 100 hours to create"
   - "This is the most beautiful sword dance ever filmed"

## Call-to-Action (CTA) Framework

### Effective CTAs for Xianxia Content

| CTA Type | Vietnamese | English | Best For |
|----------|------------|---------|----------|
| Follow | "Follow để xem tiếp!" | "Follow for Part 2!" | Episodic content |
| Comment | "Các bạn nghĩ sao?" | "What do you think?" | Engagement |
| Share | "Share cho bạn bè!" | "Share with friends!" | Viral reach |
| Next Episode | "Tập tiếp theo!" | "Part 2 coming!" | Retention |

### CTA Placement

- **Start:** Brief intro hook (1-2s)
- **Middle:** Context (don't over-explain)
- **End:** Strong CTA + cliffhanger (3-5s)

## Engagement Optimization

### Retention Drivers

| Element | Impact | How to Optimize |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| Hook | 80% of drop-offs | Make first 3s irresistible |
| Pacing | 60% retention | Cut every 2-3 seconds |
| Visual interest | 50% retention | Use dynamic camera moves |
| Sound | 40% retention | Good TTS + music |
| Mystery | 30% retention | Leave questions unanswered |

### Thumbnails & Preview Frames

For TikTok/YouTube Shorts:
- First frame = hook
- Use bold, large text
- High contrast colors
- Express emotion (shock, excitement)

## Content Performance Analysis

### Metrics to Track

| Metric | Good | Target |
|--------|------|--------|
| Like/View Ratio | >3% | >5% |
| Comment/View Ratio | >0.2% | >0.5% |
| Share/View Ratio | >0.1% | >0.3% |
| Watch Time | >50% | >70% |
| Retention | >40% | >60% |

### Viral Indicators

Content is going viral when:
- 3x normal view velocity
- Shares > comments (organic spread)
- Duet/Stitch activity
- Foreign market views (beyond primary)

## A/B Testing Framework

### Elements to Test

1. **Hook variations** — Test 2-3 different openings
2. **Duration** — 30s vs 45s vs 60s
3. **CTAs** — Different wording/placement
4. **Thumbnails** — Different preview frames

### How to Test

```markdown
## A/B Test Results — EP18 Hook

Test A: "She never expected this..." (mystery)
Test B: "POV: You found the sword" (POV)

| Metric | A (Mystery) | B (POV) |
|--------|------------|---------|
| Views | 12,500 | 18,200 |
| Retention | 55% | 68% |
| Likes | 890 (7.1%) | 1,450 (8.0%) |

Winner: B (POV hook)
```
