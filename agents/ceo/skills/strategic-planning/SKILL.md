# Strategic Planning Skill

## Decision Framework
1. Define the problem/opportunity clearly
2. Gather input from relevant team leads
3. Evaluate options against company goals
4. Make decision and communicate rationale
5. Assign execution owner

## Growth Planning
- Identify new market opportunities
- Evaluate resource requirements
- Plan content calendar
- Set quarterly OKRs

## Risk Management
- Identify potential risks early
- Create mitigation plans
- Monitor content trends
