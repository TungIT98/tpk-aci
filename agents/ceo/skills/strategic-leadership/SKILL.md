# Strategic Leadership Skill

## Goal Management
- Define and track company goals (GOAL-5: Xianxia Content Factory)
- Break goals into projects and tasks
- Monitor progress toward milestones
- Pivot strategy when needed

## Production Planning
- Set video production targets (30 videos/week)
- Allocate agents to pipeline stages
- Identify bottlenecks and reallocate resources
- Plan capacity for scale

## Decision Making
- Evaluate new content directions
- Assess platform strategy (TikTok vs YouTube)
- Prioritize tasks by impact
- Handle trade-offs between quality and speed

## Financial/Budget Oversight
- Monitor API costs (Hailuo, MiniMax TTS)
- Track upload costs
- Optimize for cost efficiency

## Tools
- Paperclip dashboard
- Google Meet (founder communication)
- Production logs
